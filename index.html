About PushUP Academy

Not just finishing the syllabus. Building understanding that lasts.

We were founded by two dedicated teachers specialising in Mathematics and Science, with a shared vision of transforming the way students learn and understand concepts. Our focus is conceptual knowledge, analytical thinking and problem-solving skills, because in-depth knowledge is the foundation of excellent academic results.

Our vision: a strong academic foundation for every student, and the push they need to reach their highest potential.

Our teaching philosophy

1Concept-based, in-depth learning
2Individual attention to every student
3Difficult ideas simplified through practical explanations
4Stronger analytical and problem-solving skills
5Regular assessments and performance monitoring
6Dedicated doubt-clearing sessions
7Special revision sessions and exam preparation
8Academic excellence and student confidence, together

Our courses

Specialised coaching in four subjects

Each subject is taught by a founder who lives and breathes it, with assessments and revision built into the schedule.

Physics

From motion and forces to electricity and modern physics, taught through derivations students can actually follow and numericals they can finish.

Taught by Geo Sebastian

Chemistry

Reactions, periodic trends, organic mechanisms and stoichiometry explained with patterns instead of rote memorisation.

Taught by Geo Sebastian

Biology

Cells, systems, genetics and ecology brought to life with diagrams, flow of processes and exam-style answer writing.

Taught by Geo Sebastian

Mathematics

Algebra to calculus, built step by step so every formula is understood before it is used, with daily practice and timed tests.

Taught by Isac Sir

Our founders

Two teachers. One shared vision.

PushUP Academy is run by the people who teach in it, so every decision about a batch, a test or a revision plan comes from the classroom.

GS

Co-founder · Physics, Chemistry & Biology

Geo Sebastian

A science educator known for making the hardest chapters feel approachable. Geo teaches all three science subjects with the same discipline: start from the concept, show how it behaves in the real world, then solve until it is second nature. Students remember his classes for the clarity of his explanations and for the way complex topics are broken into steps anyone can follow.

  • Concept-first teaching
  • Board exam specialist
  • Patient doubt clearing
IS

Co-founder · Mathematics

Isac Sir

A mathematics teacher with a reputation for turning fear of maths into fluency. Isac Sir builds every topic from its foundations, insists on understanding before shortcuts, and uses graded practice sets and timed tests so students walk into exams with confidence. His students are known for strong fundamentals and steady improvement in scores.

  • Foundations before formulas
  • Daily practice culture
  • Timed test strategy
